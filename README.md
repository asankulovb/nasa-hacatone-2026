 Ares / Artemis Outpost: Survival & Balance
Ares / Artemis Outpost: Survival & Balance is a web-based planetary engineering simulator and STEM educational platform developed in the "Altyn Tüyün" Laboratory of the Naryn branch of NDITA for the NASA Space Apps Challenge. The project places players in the role of Mission Commander overseeing the establishment and long-term viability of human habitats on the Moon (Artemis Base Alpha) and Mars (Ares Outpost Prime).
 What Is Researched and Simulated

The simulator explores real-world aerospace challenges modeled on official NASA data, physics-based formulas, and life support architectures:

1. Environmental Control and Life Support Systems (ECLSS): Simulates closed-loop oxygen recovery through the Sabatier reaction and electrolysis, carbon dioxide scrubbers ($CO_2$), and multi-stage greywater filtration.
2. Resource Thermodynamics & Power Grids: Models fluctuating solar irradiance during lunar diurnal cycles (14 Earth days of continuous night) and Martian dust storm attenuation, contrasted against baseload Kilopower nuclear reactor generators.
3. Cosmic Radiation Mitigation: Investigates ionizing deep-space galactic cosmic rays (GCR) and solar particle events (SPE), balancing active electromagnetic deflection fields with in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) regolith shielding.
4. Hydroponics and Closed-Loop Agriculture: Evaluates light spectrum requirements, nutrient cycling, and biomass yields necessary for sustained crew caloric intake and psychological stability.

 How to Play

The player's mission is to guide an expedition of specialized astronauts (engineers, botanists, and medical officers) through 30 simulated Martian or Lunar Sols.

- Power Grid Management: Each morning, allocate generated wattage among critical modules: ECLSS, Hydroponic Agrodome, and Radiation Deflector Shields. Underpowering modules triggers cascading failures—oxygen depletion, plant wilting, or radiation sickness.
- Consumable Balancing: Monitor real-time telemetry gauges tracking Energy (kWh), Oxygen ($O_2$), Water ($H_2O$), Food rations, Ambient Radiation (mSv), and Overall Crew Health (%). Daily consumption scales directly with crew size and automated operational loads.
- Operational Dilemmas: Tackle authentic orbital and planetary crises—such as regolith seal breaches, micrometeoroid impacts, dust storms, and solar proton bursts. Every decision demands balancing crew risk against critical resource expenditures.
- Technology Tree & Upgrades: Spend earned engineering points to unlock cutting-edge NASA technologies: Closed-Loop Water Recovery, Aeroponic Vertical Farms, Graphene Structural Composite Armor, and AI Diagnostic Sensors.
 The 3D STEM Academy

In addition to base survival, the platform features an interactive educational wing:
- Interactive 3D Solar System: Explore the eight planets and asteroid belt with dynamic orbital mechanics, scale models, and astrophysics telemetry.
- Orbital Habitats & NASA Discoveries: Inspect 3D models of the ISS, Lunar Gateway, and landmark exploratory missions like the James Webb Space Telescope and Perseverance Rover.
- Dynamic Examinations: Test your aerospace knowledge across randomized 15-question assessments with shuffled choices and receive an official Mission Commander Certificate upon achieving mastery.

To win, players must sustain crew health above zero and maintain operational base equilibrium through Sol 30, demonstrating true strategic mastery of extraterrestrial survival.
